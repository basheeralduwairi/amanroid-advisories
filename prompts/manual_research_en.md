# AmanRoid manual research guide

This repository does not contain an automated AI agent or an AI API call. Public
advisories are manually researched, then explicitly approved by a cybersecurity
reviewer.

Use official Jordanian or regulatory material first, preserve every original
source URL and publication date, separate confirmed Jordan incidents from global
risks, and never infer active campaign status from recency alone. Keep indicators
defanged (`hxxps://example[.]invalid`) and keep `review_status` as `draft` until
the reviewer approves the exact permanent ID.
